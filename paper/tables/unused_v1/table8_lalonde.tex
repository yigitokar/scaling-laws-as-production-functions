\begin{tablenotes}
Output is the log-odds of above-chance accuracy, $q=\ln[\tilde a/(1-\tilde a)]$ with $\tilde a=(\text{accuracy}-\text{chance})/(1-\text{chance})$. $\theta_N$ and $\theta_D$ are the coefficients on $\ln N$ and $\ln D$ in a regression of $q$ on both; $\theta_C$ is the coefficient on $\ln C=\ln 6ND$ alone. Obs.: the estimator applied to observed outputs, standard errors clustered by developer (19 clusters in Panel A). Exp.: the same estimator, with the same fixed effects and controls, applied on the same models to counterfactual outputs from the experimental technology, a quadratic surface in $(\ln N,\ln D)$ fitted to 88 designed runs (OLMo ladder with OLMo-2 7B and 13B, and Gadre et al.\ with $N\geq 0.1$B; $R^2=0.994$); standard errors from 300 bootstrap refits of the surface. Bias $=$ Obs.\ $-$ Exp.; its standard error treats the two as independent. Brackets: restricted wild-cluster bootstrap-$t$ $p$-values (Webb weights, 999 draws) for the bias, reported for the estimators and samples for which they were computed. The 57 models lie inside the convex hull of the experimental $(\ln N,\ln D)$ points (30 families, 19 developers, all with $N\le 9$B). OP-style selection: OLS with a quadratic in the estimated propensity of being an Epoch ``notable'' model. EIV: slope divided by the reliability implied by Epoch's ``Confident'' compute class. Reverse regression: inverse slope of $\ln C$ on $q$. IV: $\ln C$ instrumented by the log FLOP/s per dollar of the best data-center accelerator released at least six months before the model; $F$ is the cluster-robust first-stage statistic; in this row the Exp.\ column applies the same instrument to the experimental outputs. With developer fixed effects the same instrument has $F<0.01$ (not shown). Panel C: composite is the logit of mean chance-adjusted accuracy on HellaSwag, ARC-Challenge and Winogrande; the ladder-only surface is fitted to OLMo runs only. ARC-Challenge, Winogrande and a subsample without imputed-data, distilled, synthetic-data and code models are in Online Appendix Table~\ref{tab:app-lalonde-robust}.
