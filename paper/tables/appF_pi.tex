\begin{tablenotes}
$M^*(C)$ is never observed beyond $10^{22}$ FLOP: the largest IsoFLOP budgets are $3\times10^{21}$ (Chinchilla),
$10^{22}$ (Llama 3) and $3\times10^{20}$ (Marin; DeepSeek LLM). Anchors: each design's Approach-2 path at its largest
budget with its 95 percent wild-bootstrap interval (DeepSeek: published law). Extrapolation (Proposition~\ref{prop:pi}):
$\ln M^*(C)=\ln M^*(C_0)+e\ln(C/C_0)$ with the path elasticity $e=1-2a$ anywhere in the stated range. PI-1: range of
$e$ over the IsoFLOP paths and published laws (including DeepSeek LLM's three data sets and MiniCPM); PI-2 adds $M^*$
nondecreasing in $C$; PI-3 uses the model's own lab's anchor where it exists (Meta, Marin, DeepSeek, AI2) and PI-1
otherwise; PI-4 uses every ex-ante technology's in-support $M^*$ and all path slopes. Llama 3's 402B parameters and
16.55T tokens at $3.8\times10^{25}$ FLOP extrapolate Meta's law and are not used as an anchor. By
Proposition~\ref{prop:pi}(iii), $w>1$ is identified when $\ln M$ exceeds the upper bound for every anchor, for any
technology with single-peaked IsoFLOP profiles; no model's $w<1$ is identified in Panel B. Bounds on $s$ use
$1/\sigma^*-1\in[0.40,0.52]$, the range of the $\kappa$-free and model-free estimates, and are medians of the models'
bounds. Panel C repeats the logic on the verified sample with each model's own parameter and token conventions and
anchors fitted to the IsoFLOP minima (wild bootstrap-$t$ intervals with HC2-rescaled residuals, $M^*=20.5$ [11.4, 36.8]
at $2.9\times10^{21}$ for Chinchilla and 22.3 [12.2, 40.2] at $10^{22}$ for Llama 3); cross-sweep slopes: allocation
exponents 0.37--0.57; anchor's own slope: its 95 percent interval. It illustrates the bounds, not the paper's estimate.
\end{tablenotes}
