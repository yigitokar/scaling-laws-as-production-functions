\item \textit{Notes:} Approach 2 (conditional factor demand): per-budget parabola of $L$ in $\ln N$ on the IsoFLOP-profile runs, argmins regressed on $\ln(C/6)$. Approach 1 (inverse cost function): $L^*_b=E+K(C_b/6)^{-\gamma}$ fitted by NLS to the 9 per-budget minima (column 2 fixes $E$ at the Approach-3 value). Approach 3: Huber-LSE primal. ``Analytic'': closed forms $a=\beta/(\alpha+\beta)$, $\gamma=\alpha\beta/(\alpha+\beta)$, $N^*=G(C/6)^a$, $L^*=E+K(C/6)^{-\gamma}$. ``Design'': the Approach-2/1 procedure applied to the Approach-3 fitted values at the observed design (removes the finite-grid bias of parabola fits, \citealt{czech2026problems}). $\bar C$ = geometric mean of the IsoFLOP budgets. Wald statistics use the bootstrap covariance of the difference; for published parameter sets \citep{hoffmann2022training,besiroglu2024chinchilla} only the Approach-2/1 side is random. Path = $(a,\ln N^*(\bar C))$; frontier = $(\gamma,L^*(\bar C))$. Wald covariances are outlier-robust (IQR scales, Spearman correlations) because parabola argmins in resampled designs are heavy-tailed; SD-based versions are in the CSV. Main scheme: within-budget stratified pairs bootstrap (300 draws); the fixed-design wild bootstrap (400 Rademacher draws on the Approach-3 log residuals) is a sensitivity check (it flips the sign of the gross outliers at the $10^{19}$ budget). Last two columns: difference between the Approach-2 path slope and the null's slope, and its $t$-statistic using the classical OLS standard error of the 9-point path regression (7 d.f.; it treats the null slope as known). ``Boot.\ $p$'': bootstrap-calibrated $p$-value of the path statistic (critical value from the bootstrap distribution of the same quadratic form recentred at the estimate, instead of $\chi^2_2$), with the robust / SD covariance; the $\chi^2$ $p$-values depend strongly on the covariance choice because the resampled argmins are heavy-tailed, the calibrated ones do not. Bootstrap-free benchmark (fixed design, iid normal argmin errors): regressing the 9 argmin gaps on $[1,\ln C_b]$ and testing both coefficients, A3 (this fit) $F=0.45$ ($p=0.653$); Hoffmann $F=5.29$ ($p=0.040$); Besiroglu $F=0.43$ ($p=0.664$) ($F_{2,7}$).
