\begin{tablenotes}
Panel A: $L=E+AN^{-\alpha}+BD^{-\beta}$ estimated separately for each sweep by Huber loss ($\delta=10^{-3}$) on
$\ln L$. $s_M$: residual standard deviation of $\ln(D/N)$ given $\ln C$ (off-path design variation).
$a=\beta/(\alpha+\beta)$; $\gamma=\alpha\beta/(\alpha+\beta)$; $\sigma^*=2/(2+\alpha+\beta)$; $M^*(10^{21})$:
compute-optimal $D/N$ at $C=6ND=10^{21}$ FLOP (extrapolated where the largest budget is smaller), with 95 percent
percentile interval. Last two columns: $L=E+[AN^{-a_1}+BD^{-b_1}]^{\kappa}$, $\sigma^*_\kappa=2/(2+a_1+b_1)$;
$\kappa=1$ is rejected in every sweep ($p<0.001$, bootstrap Wald and Gaussian LR). Bootstrap standard errors in
parentheses: 400 draws resampling runs (Chinchilla, Farseer, Gadre, Muennighoff) or (size, multiplier) cells (OLMo
ladder); 200 draws for the $\kappa$ columns. Chinchilla standard errors differ slightly from Table~\ref{tab:chinchilla},
which uses separate bootstrap draws. Gadre: three web corpora of \citet{gadre2024language}; Muennighoff: single-epoch runs. DataDecide: 25 recipes pooled with common exponents and recipe-specific
$(A_r,B_r,E_r)$, estimated on 21,888 checkpoints of 1,100 runs taken before the learning-rate schedule ends, 100
draws resampling (size, seed) cells; levels are descriptive and $M^*$ is not reported. Loss units and tokenizers
differ across rows (Farseer: bits per character); $\alpha$, $\beta$, $a$, $\gamma$, $\sigma$, and $\kappa$ are unit
free. Parameter counts: non-embedding (Farseer), total (Chinchilla, Gadre), excluding the input embedding only (OLMo
ladder, DataDecide). Gaussian least squares, a pooled Gadre fit, and robustness checks are in Online Appendix Tables~\ref{tab:app-robust} and~\ref{tab:app-farseer}.
Panel B: Chinchilla form by Huber loss; $\kappa$ family and Farseer's nine-parameter form (Eq.~3 of
\citealt{li2025predictableb}) by Gaussian least squares, except $\sigma^*_\kappa$ (Huber, as in Panel A). Held-out
errors: models refitted without the top decile of $6ND$ or without the four largest model sizes, from
training-sample starting values. Local quadratic: Gaussian-kernel local quadratic of $\ln L$ in $(\ln N,\ln D)$ at 42
interior points, with a bootstrap interval for the median; $\sigma$ in the Farseer form is evaluated at the same
points.
\end{tablenotes}
