\begin{tablenotes}
Seven sweep--corpus technologies from five studies. Outer exponent: $L=E+(AN^{-a_1}+BD^{-b_1})^\kappa$ fitted by Huber
loss on $\ln L$, pairs-bootstrap standard errors (m2 estimates; design-conditional wild standard errors are in Online
Appendix Table~\ref{tab:app-kappa-robust}); $p(\kappa=1)$: Wald. CES: Wald test of $\alpha=\beta$ in the Chinchilla
form with Huber loss, and the Gaussian likelihood-ratio (LR) test against $\chi^2_1$. The corresponding $\sigma^*_\kappa$ are in Online Appendix Table~\ref{tab:app-kappa-robust}. $^a$Heavy tails make the Gaussian LR
miscalibrated on Chinchilla: its restricted-bootstrap $p$-value is 0.17 and heteroskedasticity-robust Wald $p$-values are
0.060 (HC1) and 0.089 (HC3) (Online Appendix Table~\ref{tab:app-chin-inference}). Rank one: bootstrap test that the
second-order matrix of a translog in $\ln(L-\hat E)$ has rank one, as the $\kappa$ family implies, with $\hat E$ from the
Chinchilla form or from the $\kappa$ family; 300 null draws, so the smallest attainable $p$ is 0.003. Rank-one curvature is rejected at 5 percent in two
of seven sweeps with $\hat E_{\kappa=1}$ and in one (Farseer) with $\hat E_\kappa$.
