\begin{tablenotes}Lifetime cost $6N^{1+\delta}D+2\varrho N^{\phi}T$ in training-FLOP units at the model's own size: $\delta$ is the elasticity of the training cost of a FLOP with respect to $N$ (model FLOPs utilization falling or rising with size), $\phi$ the elasticity of serving cost per token with respect to $N$, and $\varrho$ the price of a serving FLOP relative to a training FLOP. The first-order condition is $w=1+\delta+\phi X_S/X_T$, with $X_S$ and $X_T$ serving and training expenditure, so $X_S/X_T=(w-1-\delta)/\phi$, $s=(w-1-\delta)/(w-1-\delta+\phi)$ and $T/D=3(w-1-\delta)/(\varrho\phi)$. Case A: $\phi=1+\delta$, so that $w=(1+\delta)(1+\varrho T/3D)$. Case B: $\delta=0$. The share $s$ does not depend on $\varrho$. Clean sample, reference technology; $T/D$ is the serving reading of the wedge (Proposition~\ref{prop:A-wedge}(iii)(a)), which requires that the developer bears the serving cost.\end{tablenotes}
