Panel C: condition number of $J'J$ at the truth with $A,B$ normalized at the geometric means of $N,D$; Wald = LAD-sandwich/delta-method 95\% CI coverage for $\sigma^*$ ($^{\dagger}$: covariance numerically singular or corner in $>5\%$ of replications; coverage over computable replications); bootstrap = pairs-bootstrap percentile CI (149 draws, first 150 replications), each draw warm-started at the replication's estimate ($^{\S}$: in a separate check on 60 fresh replications with 49 draws that also restarts every draw from the estimator's nine random starting values, on-path ($v=0.02$): 33\% warm-start vs 78\% multi-start coverage (median widths 0.042 vs 0.426); $v=0.3$: 100\% warm-start vs 100\% multi-start coverage (median widths 0.053 vs 0.053)); profile CI = median width of the 95\% profile-likelihood CI for $\sigma^*$ in the $\kappa$-generalized model $L=E+(AN^{-a_1}+BD^{-b_1})^{\kappa}$ with $\kappa$ free, on the grid $[0.05,0.99]$ (first 150 replications), inverting LR$=n\ln(\mathrm{SSR}(\sigma^*)/\mathrm{SSR}_{\min})$ against the $\chi^2_1$ critical value, with $\mathrm{SSR}_{\min}$ the unrestricted ($\kappa$ and $\sigma^*$ free) minimum; `full' = the median CI is the entire grid; CI endpoints are linear interpolations of LR between grid points (spacing 0.004--0.005 next to the truth); flat = share of replications whose profile CI is the entire grid. Dual estimator: frontier fitted from a $2\times2\times2$ grid of starting values (none at the truth).
