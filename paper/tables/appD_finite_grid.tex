\begin{tablenotes}
Nine equally spaced runs in $\ln N$, centered at the minimum of the \citet{besiroglu2024chinchilla} IsoFLOP profile at
$10^{21}$ FLOP, without noise; true $\sigma^*=0.7370$. The estimator is $1/\hat\sigma^*-1=\hat L_{nn}/(2|dL^*/dc|)$ with
$\hat L_{nn}$ twice the quadratic (or quartic) coefficient and the exact frontier slope. Leading-order bias of the
quadratic coefficient: $(f^{(4)}/24)\,\kappa_4$ with $\kappa_4=(m_6-m_2m_4)/(m_4-m_2^2)$ and $m_k$ the grid's $k$-th
moment; argmin bias $-(f'''/6)(m_4/m_2)/f''$ (Online Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}, remark after
Proposition~\ref{prop:A-modelfree}). The table reports bias only: with seed and evaluation noise, quartic fits and
narrow grids have higher variance.
