\begin{tablenotes}
$N$ is the parameter count in billions (active parameters for the mixture-of-experts model$^{\ddagger}$); $D$ is
training tokens in trillions; $M=D/N$. $\hat w=\varepsilon_N/\varepsilon_D$ at the model's $(N,D)$ under the reference
technology (Huber refit of the Chinchilla extraction; Section~\ref{sec:tech}), with a 95 percent pairs-bootstrap interval
(400 draws). $\hat T/D=3(\hat w-1)$ is planned lifetime inference tokens per training token, and $\hat w-1$ is planned
lifetime inference compute as a multiple of training compute; negative values are not inference demand. CE
$=C_{\min}(L)/(6ND)$ is Farrell cost efficiency relative to the training-only frontier. Technology band: union of the
95 percent intervals (or point estimates) for $\hat w$ under the Chinchilla refit, \citet{besiroglu2024chinchilla},
\citet{hoffmann2022training} (Approach 3), Farseer with $N$ including and excluding embeddings, and \citet{gadre2024language}
(RefinedWeb), plus the developer's own technologies for Meta and AI2 models; it is a sensitivity envelope, not a
confidence set. Own law: $\hat w$ under Meta's Llama~3 IsoFLOP law (Approach 2 with $\kappa=1$) for Meta models and under
AI2's OLMo ladder for OLMo~2. $^{f}$Flagship (largest model of its family). $^{\dagger}$Distilled from a larger model.
$^{c}$/$^{e}$: $M$ above the largest ratio in the Chinchilla design (341) / also above that in the Farseer design with
embeddings counted (1,227). Llama~3 and Llama~3.1 8B and 70B have identical $N$ and $D$.
