\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\footnotesize
\item \textit{Notes:} 400 replications per scenario. Simulated industry: 40 labs $\times$ 6 generations, 1--4 models per lab-generation (tier compute offsets $\approx 8\times$), Chinchilla technology at the Besiroglu et al.\ (2024) truth, Hicks-neutral productivity $\omega_{ft}=\delta_t+x_{ft}$, $x_{ft}=0.8x_{f,t-1}+\xi_{ft}$, sd$(x)=0.25$, TFP growth $0.15$ per generation; seed noise sd $0.05$ in $y=-\ln(L-E)$. Compute rules: (a) budget independent of $\omega$; (b) $c_{ft}$ rises by $2x_{ft}$; (c) labs buy the compute that reaches a loss target; (d) $c_{ft}$ rises by $2x_{f,t-1}$ (provisioned a generation ahead). Allocation: lifetime-compute optimum with log-normal inference demand $T$ (mean $\ln w\approx0.46$) plus allocation error (sd $0.2$ in $\ln D/N$). $E$ is known to every estimator except the pooled Huber-LSE with $E$ free (ML practice). TFP growth and sd$(\omega)$ are computed from lab-generation means of $y-\hat F(n,d)$. Both ACF rows use current within-lab-generation deviations of $n$ and $d$ as instruments (valid here because tier offsets and inference demand are independent of $\omega$); without them the lagged-instrument moment set is under-identified. `--': not identified in that design. $^{\ddagger}$Just-identified 2SLS has no finite moments; for this row the table reports the median bias and the median absolute error instead of the mean bias and the RMSE. Mundlak (CRE) estimates are close to lab FE, and the Heckman estimator coincides with pooled NLS when there is no selection; both are reported in the appendix version of this table.
