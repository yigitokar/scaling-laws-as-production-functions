(2020) form ($q=\alpha_D\approx0.1$); isoquants depend only on the inner exponents, so $\sigma^*_q=2/(2+\alpha'+\beta')$ (95\% bootstrap interval in brackets, 200 draws); Wald uses the bootstrap standard error of $\hat q$ (Huber-LSE), LR uses Gaussian NLS. Rank-one curvature: $\hat\tau=\det H/\lVert H\rVert_F^2$ for the Hessian $H$ of an OLS translog of $\ln(L-\hat E)$ on $(\ln N,\ln D)$; Chinchilla and the $q$ family both imply a rank-one Hessian ($\tau=0$) whose null direction is the expansion path, but each with its own $E$. Left pair: $\hat E$ from the Chinchilla fit, null distribution from a residual bootstrap under the fitted Chinchilla model; right pair: $\hat E$ from the $q$ family, null from a residual bootstrap under the fitted $q$ model ($E$ re-estimated in every draw; 300 draws each). Because an error in $\hat E$ adds a constant to reducible loss, the left pair rejects whenever $q\neq1$ even if curvature is rank one (a noise-free $q$-family surface on the Farseer design gives $\hat\tau=-0.27$ with the Chinchilla $\hat E$); the right pair is the test of rank-one curvature. Pairs-bootstrap 95\% intervals of $\hat\tau$ in brackets; $p$ is two-sided relative to the null distribution ($\leq$ marks the smallest attainable value, $1/(B+1)$). Null medians and ranges are in the CSV file.
