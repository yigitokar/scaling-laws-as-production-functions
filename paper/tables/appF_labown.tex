\begin{tablenotes}
Panel A: $\sigma^*$ used is the model-free random-effects estimate of Section~\ref{sec:tech} over budgets whose minimum
is bracketed by two runs on each side (Online Appendix Table~\ref{tab:app-mf-sens}). Global quadratics: a second
estimator that fits one quadratic to all runs of each budget, including unbracketed ones, and pools a ratio of sums;
design-conditional wild bootstrap, 999 draws. Its simulation-based bias correction gives 0.662--0.678, and it is used
only in sensitivity rows; for Chinchilla it uses the 123-point snapped subset of the IsoFLOP compilation of \citet{czech2026problems} (eight budgets) as a cross-check. Path slope and $M^*$: Approach-2 argmins regressed on $\ln C$ over all budgets; brackets:
wild bootstrap. Meta's path reproduces the published law $D^*=0.299\,C^{0.537}$; fitted on its eight bracketed budgets
it has slope 0.501 and $M^*(10^{24})=15$ instead of 31. Panel B: lab-own technologies are Meta's IsoFLOP path with the
model-free curvature; AI2's OLMo ladder with $\kappa$ free; DeepSeek LLM's published allocation law in its own units
with the reference curvature (no curvature published); and Marin's Nemotron-CC path with the model-free curvature.
Vintage: whether the lab's law was published before the model was planned (contemporaneous) or afterward (ex post).
Llama 3.1 405B lies on Meta's path by construction, because Meta sized it with this law. Alternatives: Meta's $\kappa=1$
law, primal and path on eight budgets; AI2's $\kappa=1$ ladder; Marin's other corpora and $\kappa=1$ rows. Reference:
Chinchilla with $\kappa$ free. Medians are over the 22 models with a lab-own technology (Meta 10, AI2 9, DeepSeek 2,
Marin 1).
