\item \textit{Notes:} 400 replications per scenario. Simulated industry: 40 labs $\times$ 6 generations, 1--4 models per lab-generation (tier compute offsets $\approx 8\times$), Chinchilla technology at the Besiroglu et al.\ (2024) truth, Hicks-neutral productivity $\omega_{ft}=\delta_t+x_{ft}$, $x_{ft}=0.8x_{f,t-1}+\xi_{ft}$, sd$(x)=0.25$, TFP growth $0.15$ per generation; seed noise sd $0.05$ in $y=-\ln(L-E)$. Compute rules: (a) budget independent of $\omega$; (b) $c_{ft}$ rises by $2x_{ft}$; (c) labs buy the compute that reaches a loss target; (d) $c_{ft}$ rises by $2x_{f,t-1}$ (provisioned a generation ahead). Allocation: lifetime-compute optimum with log-normal inference demand $T$ (mean $\ln w\approx0.46$) plus allocation error (sd $0.2$ in $\ln D/N$). $E$ is known to every estimator except the pooled Huber-LSE with $E$ free (ML practice). TFP growth and sd$(\omega)$ are computed from lab-generation means of $y-\hat F(n,d)$. Both ACF rows use current within-lab-generation deviations of $n$ and $d$ as instruments (valid here because tier offsets and inference demand are independent of $\omega$); without them the lagged-instrument moment set is under-identified. `--': not identified in that design. $^{\ddagger}$Just-identified 2SLS has no finite moments; for this row the table reports the median bias and the median absolute error instead of the mean bias and the RMSE. Selection: a model is released only if its $y$ beats the lab's previous same-tier model by $0.5$ or clears the generation median (open-weight labs: bar lowered by $0.25$); about 18\% of trained models are not released. The Heckman correction uses all trained models' inputs and the open-weight policy as the exclusion restriction. ME: classical error (sd $0.15$) in $\ln D$, with reported $C=6ND$. $\psi_D$: lab-specific data-augmenting productivity (sd $0.3$) that the labs' allocation reflects. Flagships: one model per lab-generation under the funding rule; within-family estimators are not identified.
