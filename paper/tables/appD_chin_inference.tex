\begin{tablenotes}
Epoch AI's digitization of the Chinchilla runs: 245 runs, 137 on nine IsoFLOP profiles and 108 off-profile runs in
35 model-size trunks, training FLOP $1.4\times10^{18}$--$1.3\times10^{22}$; the five highest-loss runs are dropped
($n=240$: 132 profile runs). Estimator: Huber loss ($\delta=10^{-3}$) on $\ln L$. Panel A: 999 draws per scheme;
standard errors in parentheses, percentile intervals in brackets. Nine budgets: every run assigned to the nearest
IsoFLOP budget; budgets, trunks: profile runs clustered by budget and off-profile runs by model size; wild: design
held fixed, $y^*=\hat y+v|\tilde r|$ around the $\kappa$-free fit with the weights and leverage-adjusted residuals of
\citet{feng2011wild}; wild cluster: one weight of \citet{webb2023reworking} per cluster. $p$-values: Wald with the
scheme's standard error. The standard error of $\alpha$ is 0.014--0.025 across schemes. Panel B: $p_{\rm boot}$ from a
restricted wild (Feng--He--Hu) bootstrap that generates the data under the null, 999 draws (smallest attainable $p$ =
0.001); heteroskedasticity-robust Wald tests of $\alpha=\beta$ for the Gaussian fit give $p$ = 0.060 (HC1) and 0.089
(HC3). Panel C: $\sigma^*=2/(2+a_1+b_1)$ in the $\kappa$ family, grid $[0.05,0.99]$; on-path band: runs with $|\ln N-\ln N^*(C)|\le0.15$.
Restricted-bootstrap 95 percent critical values of the Laplace quasi-LR are 7.7--11.4 on the full design (299 draws
each), which widens its set to [0.68, 0.72].
\end{tablenotes}
