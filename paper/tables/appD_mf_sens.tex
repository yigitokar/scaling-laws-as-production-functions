\begin{tablenotes}
Model-free $\sigma^*$ from $1/\sigma^*-1=L_{nn}|_C/(2|dL^*/dc|)$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:modelfree}), estimated per
IsoFLOP budget from a local polynomial in $\ln N$ on runs within $h$ of a pooled Approach-2 path (primary: quadratic,
$h=1$; log-cubic frontier in $\ln C$, log-quadratic for Marin Comma) and pooled across budgets by a DerSimonian--Laird
random-effects (RE) mean \citep{dersimonian1986meta}. Budgets are valid if the minimum is interior with at least two runs on
each side: 9/9 (Chinchilla), 8/10 (Llama 3), 5/7, 7/7, 7/8 (Marin) and 12/12 (Porian). Standard errors: design-conditional
wild bootstrap with budget-level shocks, 999 draws (199 for Panel A's variants). Global quadratic: all runs of a budget.
Frontier smoother: log-quadratic, cubic in levels and power-law frontiers. Iterated window: centered on each budget's own
argmin. Independent code: an equal-weighted re-implementation that shares no code with the primary. RW:
RefinedWeb; OWT2: OpenWebText2. Pooled $F$ tests of a cubic term never reject at 5
percent ($p$ from 0.055 to 0.85). Marin with configuration $N$ instead of $C_b/(6D)$: 0.658, 0.642 and 0.655. Porian et
al.'s profiles are read off constant-learning-rate runs without cooldown. Panel B: 100 replications with 149 bootstrap draws each; the truth is each design's fitted
$\kappa$-family technology plus Gaussian noise at the design's robust residual standard deviation; pooled: ratio of
weighted sums with a basic bootstrap interval. Noise-free bias: range over budgets under that technology without noise.
\end{tablenotes}
