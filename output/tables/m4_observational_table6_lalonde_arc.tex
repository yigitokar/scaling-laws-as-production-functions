The benchmark differs across estimators because each estimator weights the local elasticities of a non-log-linear technology differently. Compute-only rows: IV instruments $\ln C$ with the log FLOP/s per dollar of the best data-centre accelerator released six months before the model (or of the model's own training hardware, Epoch); F = cluster-robust first-stage F.
