\begin{tablenotes}[Notes]
Panel A: $a$, allocation exponent ($N^*\propto C^{a}$; for Farseer's own form, the local slope at $10^{22}$--$10^{23}$
FLOP). Growth of $D^*$ at frontier compute growth of 5.06-fold per year. $D^*$ in 2026: compute-optimal data at the
fitted frontier compute of September 2026 ($9.4\times10^{26}$ FLOP), in trillions (T) of each technology's own tokens.
Years: first date at which frontier runs reach the quality-adjusted (100T) or repetition-adjusted (320T) stock of public
text, growing 5 percent per year, under compute-optimal allocation ($D^*(C)$) or at the median $D/D^*(C)$ of the four
disclosed dense runs above $10^{25}$ FLOP (obs.\ $M$). Panel B: $r=D^*(C)/U$; extra compute to reach the unconstrained
frontier loss; $r_{\max}$, scarcity beyond which that loss is unattainable; shadow value, $-\partial C/\partial U$ at
fixed loss in units of the cost of processing one token; $\sigma_{CU}$, elasticity of substitution between compute and
unique tokens; $\gamma_{\rm eff}/\gamma$, compute elasticity of reducible loss behind the wall relative to the frontier
elasticity. Equivalent: members of the $\kappa=1$ refit's observationally equivalent family. Panel C:
$s=\sum_i(w_i^+-1)C_i/\sum_iw_i^+C_i$, $w^+=\max(w,1)$, under the reference technology unless stated; open-weight
universe: models with $6ND\geq10^{21}$ FLOP and confident $N$ and $D$, excluding mixture-of-experts and non-transformer
models; 95 percent CI from a design-conditional wild bootstrap ($B=399$); Range: minimum and maximum over the 32 ex-ante
technologies.
