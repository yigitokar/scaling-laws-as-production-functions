\begin{tablenotes}Models: models/families. Compute-equivalent 90/10 ratios $\exp[(\omega_{90}-\omega_{10})/\theta_C]$: the factor by which the

$\omega_f$ come from a regression of the output on $\ln N$, $\ln D$, and family fixed effects; the ``$\ln C$'' column
instead nets inputs with the experimental compute elasticity ($\omega_f$ = family mean of $y-\theta_C\ln C$), so that

draws, holding the experimental $\theta$ fixed. Loss units raise the family ratio (experimental $\theta$) to the
