Years: first date at which frontier runs reach the quality-adjusted stock of public text (100T tokens in 2024, 95\% CI [22T, 490T]) or the repetition-adjusted stock (320T [65T, 1,700T]) of Villalobos et al. (2024), growing 5\% per year; `observed $M$' scales each technology's $D^*$ by the median ratio $D/D^*(C)$ of the four disclosed dense (non-mixture-of-experts) runs above $10^{25}$ FLOP (2024--2026). Panel B: technologies combine the Chinchilla family with the repetition model of Muennighoff et al. (2023), $D'=U+UR^*_D(1-e^{-R/R^*_D})$, with their jointly fitted $R^*_D=15.4$ but without their decay of excess parameters (the most favourable case; the last two rows use their full model and their data-only fit); `equivalent members' share the $\kappa=1$ refit's expansion path and frontier and differ only in curvature. $r=D^*(C)/U$ is the ratio of compute-optimal to unique tokens. Extra compute: additional FLOP needed to reach the unconstrained frontier loss. $r_{\max}$: scarcity beyond which that loss is unattainable at any compute. Shadow value: $-\partial C/\partial U$ at fixed loss, in units of the processing cost of one token ($6N$); at $C=10^{26}$ one unit is about \$7 per million tokens at \$$1.0\times10^{-18}$ per FLOP (median amortized cost of 2024--2025 frontier runs, Epoch AI). $\sigma_{CU}$: elasticity of substitution between compute and unique tokens along the isoquant. $\gamma_{\rm eff}/\gamma$: elasticity of reducible loss with respect to compute behind the wall relative to the frontier elasticity. All Panel B objects depend on $r$ only (checked at $10^{25}$ and $10^{27}$ FLOP). Panel C: module ra2's inversion. $s=\sum_i(w_i^+-1)C_i/\sum_i w_i^+C_i$ with $w^+=\max(w,1)$ (compute-weighted; planned inference truncated at 0), under ra2's reference technology (Chinchilla refit, $\kappa$ free, $\sigma^*=0.70$). Universe: production-scale ($6ND\geq10^{21}$) open-weight models with confident $N$ and $D$, excluding mixture-of-experts, non-transformer and ra2-excluded models. 95\% CI: design-conditional wild bootstrap of the reference technology ($B=399$). $\kappa=1$: the Chinchilla refit with $\kappa=1$. Clean verified sample: ra2's 77-model inference-demand sample (2023--2025); technology range: minimum and maximum of the aggregate recomputed under each of ra2's 32 ex-ante technologies. Growth calibration: halving reducible loss requires $2^{1/\gamma}=49\times$ compute at $\gamma=0.178$ (2.4 years of frontier compute growth).
